Rapid Wall PANEL Installation

Renacon wall panel are a safe and sturdy solution for your home. These solid wall panels are made up of two fibre reinforced cement sheets that are neatly placed on either side of a lightweight concrete core to balance its weight with the occupied space. Meticulously designed to occupy less space while being lightweight, these panels are manufactured to be eco-friendly and sustainable.
